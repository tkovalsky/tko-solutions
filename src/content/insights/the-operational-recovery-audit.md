The program is late and its teams offer different explanations. Before deciding whether to add funding, narrow scope, or change delivery partners, establish what the next release actually requires.

A plan and a status report are useful inputs. Test their claims against the operating and technical artifacts that must connect.

## Define what ready means

Choose one bounded change and the population it affects. Identify the approved rule, systems, operational owners, exceptions, and downstream behavior.

Engineering completion, staff preparation, policy approval, and an accepted cross-system handoff are different forms of evidence. The release definition should say which are required and how they will be demonstrated.

## Find the decisions the work is waiting for

Record each unresolved decision, the options, affected work, accountable decision-maker, and date it is needed. Separate a dependency that has an owner from work that belongs to no team.

An independent readout can make the gap visible. It does not grant a consultant authority to make the client's policy, clinical, funding, or staffing decision.

## Reconstruct the critical handoffs

For each handoff, inspect the input, controlling rule, emitted artifact, receiving system, acknowledgement, and failure behavior. Include missing information and rejected records in the walkthrough.

The [synthetic healthcare lab](/healthcare/impact-assessment) illustrates why a correct policy determination can still need separate claims-control and integration evidence. It is a teaching model, not a real client recovery case.

## Give the sponsor a decision package

The output should distinguish what is demonstrated, what is missing, which assumptions remain open, and the work required for each option. Options can include proceeding with a smaller release, resolving a prerequisite, rescoping, or reconsidering the program. Do not prescribe an arbitrary scope cut or launch date before the evidence exists.

The [Readiness Sprint](/services/regulatory-implementation-readiness-sprint) follows one approved healthcare mandate or platform transition over 3–4 weeks for $15,000 fixed. It produces the obligation register, cross-system impacts, gaps and owners, implementation backlog, and executive readiness readout. The sponsor retains the recovery decision.
