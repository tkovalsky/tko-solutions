Your team can do the work, but routine requests keep waiting for you. The issue may be missing authority, missing context, a genuine capacity constraint, or a combination. Inspect the actual decisions before choosing an automation.

A request for a discount, a changed delivery date, and an exception to a client-contact policy are different decisions. Each needs a defined owner and boundary.

## The three parts of a decision

**Context:** What is known, where did it come from, and what remains uncertain?

**Judgment:** Which choice requires interpretation or expertise?

**Authority:** Who may decide, within what approved limits, and what must be escalated?

The team needs all three. A procedure that explains the steps without granting authority can still leave every exception on the principal's desk.

## Document approved tolerances

Use recent requests to draft a decision matrix. State the permitted action, required inputs, responsible role, approval limit, and exception route. Leadership approves the actual tolerances; example percentages from an article are not a policy.

Test the boundary with scenarios on either side of it. Include missing, conflicting, and stale information. Record how the operator recognizes an exception and who accepts it.

## Automate enforcement where the rule is explicit

A deterministic rule can check whether a request meets an approved boundary. Missing information should produce a visible question or escalation. A model can prepare a summary or draft; it should not invent a fact or authorize a change to a client record.

For a fictional pricing request, the system gathers the approved terms and flags the requested variation. The authorized person reviews the proposal. Only the configured action path may apply the approved change, with its outcome recorded. That is a design example, not a deployed TKO billing integration.

## Measure the decision queue

Count requests by type, time waiting, rework, and owner. Measure the change after the new boundary is in use. Do not assume that a fixed share of decisions will disappear.
