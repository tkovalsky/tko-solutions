### Diagnostic credit decision

An automatic $7,500 credit against a $7,500 build leaves no additional build revenue. A $15,000 build after a $7,500 credited diagnostic produces $15,000 total revenue for both phases; at the assumed 25 + 50 hours, that is $200 per delivery hour before costs. A standalone diagnostic followed by a separately priced $15,000 build produces $22,500 across those assumed 75 hours, or $300 per delivery hour.

Implemented policy: the diagnostic stands alone, no automatic credit, and scope reuse is reflected explicitly in the build proposal. Existing signed terms remain valid. Removed the public founding-client discount and any coupling between fees and case-study permission. Direct-to-build remains available when the problem and scope are already understood.

Monthly support names included systems, total allowance, business hours, response target, usage costs, escalation, and cancellation terms; unused or overage rules are written before signing.
