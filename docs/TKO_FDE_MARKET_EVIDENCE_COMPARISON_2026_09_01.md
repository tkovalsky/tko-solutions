# TKO FDE Market-Evidence Comparison

**Date:** 2026-09-01  
**Sources compared:** UHC/FDE market-evidence master prompt and the reconciled TKO P0 positioning audit  
**Decision rule:** The reconciled audit governs the current public site. The FDE framework informs capability evidence, career positioning, authority development, and future content.

## Executive verdict

The two documents are compatible only when they are assigned different jobs.

- The **reconciled audit** protects the public advisory business from another broad positioning migration.
- The **FDE prompt** identifies an emerging market capability: a senior healthcare operator who can cross discovery, operating model, product, architecture, applied AI, implementation, adoption, and outcomes.

TKO should demonstrate more of that lifecycle over time. It should not rename the public practice around UHC, GL30/GL31, Forward-Deployed Engineering, Applied AI, or systems of action.
