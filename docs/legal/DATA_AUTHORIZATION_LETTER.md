# Data authorization letter (template)

Draft October 18, 2026. Not legal advice; counsel to review the warranty language and whether the Client's brokerage or firm must co-sign. Signed before Provider accepts any export, CSV, or API key.

---

**To:** [Provider legal name], d/b/a TKO Solutions / SidecarOS
**From:** [Client legal name], [license or registration number], [brokerage or firm], [address]
**Date:** [date]
**Re:** Authorization to access and process contact data

1. **Authority.** I confirm that I own, or am licensed and permitted by my brokerage/firm to use and export, the contact database described below, and that providing it to Provider for the Services does not violate my brokerage/firm agreement, any CRM terms of service, or any agreement with a third party.

2. **Data described.** Source system: [Follow Up Boss / Wealthbox / Redtail / CSV export from ____]. Approximate contact count: [____]. Date range: [____]. Fields included: [name, email, phone, address, stage, tags, notes, activity, custom fields: ____].

3. **Access method.** [ ] I will create an API key / OAuth connection in my own account and enter it in Provider's secure form. [ ] I will upload a CSV through Provider's secure form. I will not send credentials or files by email or text.

4. **Consent basis and suppression.** The opt-out / do-not-contact source of truth for these contacts is [system/field]. I will keep it current and tell Provider about any complaint within one business day. I understand Provider will not email any contact lacking a dated relationship and will never send SMS.

5. **Holdout.** I agree that [10 / 15 / 20] percent of each segment will be withheld from Provider-prepared sequences for 90 days to measure results. I may still contact those people myself.

6. **Sensitive data.** The export does not knowingly include Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, health information, or protected-characteristic data. If any is found, Provider may drop the field and will tell me.

7. **Licensed and regulated activity.** I understand Provider is not a licensed real-estate broker or sales associate and not a registered investment adviser; all substantive conversations with contacts will be conducted by me or my licensed/registered personnel.

8. **For registered investment advisers only.** My Chief Compliance Officer, [name], has reviewed this engagement under our service-provider oversight procedures and will approve all templates before use. Our archiving vendor is [vendor]; all client-facing sends will originate from our systems.

9. **Revocation.** I may revoke this authorization in writing; Provider will stop processing within one business day and complete export and deletion per the Data Processing Terms.

Signed: ______________________  Name: ____________  Title: ____________
