# Engagement Kit

Everything needed to turn a "yes" into a signed, paid, delivered Constraint Diagnostic, and the
diagnostic into a build proposal. Terms and prices follow `docs/POSITIONING_LOCK.md`.

## From "yes" to paid

1. **Proposal:** fill in `DIAGNOSTIC_PROPOSAL.md` (one page) and send it as a PDF within 24 hours
   of the call.
2. **Terms:** attach `CONSULTING_TERMS.md`. ⚠️ This is a draft. Have a Florida business attorney
   review it once before the first signature; expect a fixed fee of a few hundred dollars.
3. **Signature:** any e-signature tool (Dropbox Sign, DocuSign, or PandaDoc free tiers work).
4. **Payment:** send a Stripe or QuickBooks invoice or payment link for 100% of the diagnostic.
   The work starts when payment clears.
5. **Kickoff:** schedule interviews using `INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md`.

## Delivering the diagnostic

| Step | File | Your time (estimate) |
|---|---|---|
| Kickoff call with the sponsor | `INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md` §1 | 1 h |
| Up to 6 staff conversations | `INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md` §2 | 4.5 h |
| System walkthrough and record sample | `WORKFLOW_MAP.md` | 4 h |
| Baseline numbers | `BASELINE_WORKSHEET.md` | 2 h |
| Analysis and the diagnosis report | `DIAGNOSIS_REPORT.md` | 7 h |
| Build proposal | `BUILD_PROPOSAL.md` | 2 h |
| Readout | `DIAGNOSIS_REPORT.md` | 1.5 h |
| **Total** | | **About 22 hours** |

**With a full-time job:** 22 hours over two weeks is about 11 hours a week. Hold conversations in
early-morning or lunch slots, and do the analysis in two evening blocks plus one weekend block.
If that's too tight, quote **three weeks**. Never promise turnaround you can't keep. Take one
diagnostic at a time until the first one is delivered.

## After the readout

- **Ask for the referral** at the readout, while the value is clearest.
- **Ask for case-study permission** separately, in writing. It's optional and never tied to price.
- Log the outcome in `content/outreach/target-accounts.csv`.
