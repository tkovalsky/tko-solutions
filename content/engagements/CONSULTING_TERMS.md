# TKO Solutions Consulting Terms

> ⚠️ **DRAFT, not legal advice.** Have a Florida business attorney review this once before the
> first client signs. Confirm the governing-law venue and liability cap, and that these terms work
> alongside your employment agreement.

These terms apply to every proposal or statement of work ("Proposal") between TKO Solutions
("TKO") and the client named in it ("Client"). If a Proposal conflicts with these terms, the
Proposal wins for that engagement.

## 1. Services

TKO provides the services described in the Proposal. Anything not described there is out of
scope and requires a written change, which may change the price or timeline.

## 2. Fees and payment

- **Diagnostics** are paid 100% before work begins.
- **Builds** are paid 50% at signing and 50% on delivery, unless the Proposal says otherwise.
- **Monthly services** are paid in advance each month.

Invoices are due on receipt; ACH and card are accepted. Balances unpaid after 15 days may pause
the work. Fees exclude taxes and third-party costs (software subscriptions, messaging, hosting,
AI usage), which the Client pays directly unless the Proposal says otherwise.

## 3. Client responsibilities

The Client provides timely access to people, information, and systems as described in the
Proposal, and a sponsor with authority to make decisions. Delays caused by missing access may
move the timeline.

## 4. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential, uses it only for the
engagement, and returns or deletes it on request. This does not cover information that is
public, already known, or independently developed, or disclosure required by law.

## 5. Sensitive data

The Client will not share protected health information, payment card data, or government
identifiers unless the Proposal expressly provides for it and any agreement the law requires
(such as a business associate agreement) has been signed first.

## 6. Ownership

- **Deliverables:** on full payment, the Client owns the deliverables created specifically for
  it: reports, specifications, and the code and configuration built for the Client.
- **TKO's materials:** TKO keeps its pre-existing tools, templates, methods, and general know-how.
  Where they are part of a deliverable, the Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive license to
  use them for its own business.
- **Third-party software** remains subject to its own license.

## 7. Publicity

TKO will not name the Client or publish its results without the Client's separate written
permission. Permission is optional and never a condition of price.

## 8. No guarantee of outcomes

TKO will perform the services with professional care. TKO does not guarantee specific business
results, such as revenue, conversion, or savings. AI-assisted features operate under the
human-review rules agreed in the Proposal.

## 9. Limitation of liability

Except for breach of confidentiality or a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct:

- each party's total liability is limited to the fees paid under the Proposal in the 12 months
  before the claim; and
- neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, including lost
  profits.

## 10. Term and termination

Either party may end an engagement with 10 days' written notice. The Client pays for work
performed and non-cancellable costs incurred up to the termination date. A prepaid diagnostic
is refundable pro rata for work not yet performed. Monthly services end at the close of the paid
month.

## 11. Independent contractor

TKO is an independent contractor. Nothing here creates employment, partnership, or agency.

## 12. General

These terms and the Proposal are the entire agreement for the engagement. They are governed by
the laws of the State of Florida, with venue in {county} County, Florida. Changes must be in
writing and signed or approved by email by both parties.
