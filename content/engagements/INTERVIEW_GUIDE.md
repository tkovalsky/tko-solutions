# Interview Guide: Constraint Diagnostic

Goal: find where the work in scope actually stalls, who is compensating by hand, and what it
costs. Listen for the work behind the process, not the process document.

**Rules:** about 45 minutes each. One person at a time where possible. Ask for examples, not
opinions ("Show me the last one"). Take notes in the person's words. Never record without
permission. Don't collect customer personal data; screen-share instead of exporting.

## 1. Sponsor kickoff (60 minutes)

1. In one sentence, what outcome is this workflow supposed to produce?
2. What made you take this call now? What happened?
3. If this were fixed six months from now, what would be different? How would you know?
4. Where do you think it breaks? (Write it down. It's a hypothesis to test, not the answer.)
5. What has been tried already: tools, hires, agencies? What happened?
6. Who are the 4–6 people closest to this work? Who is the person everyone asks?
7. What can't change: tools under contract, people, regulations, budget?
8. What number would you trust as a baseline? Does it exist today?

## 2. Staff conversations (45 minutes each)

**Their work**
1. Walk me through the last time a {lead / request / job} came to you. Start to finish.
2. Where did it come from? How did you know it was yours?
3. What did you do next? Which tools did you open?

**Where it stalls**
4. When does one of these sit and wait? For what or whom?
5. What do you have to remember that no system reminds you of?
6. Where do you copy information from one place to another?
7. Which decisions do you have to ask someone else about? Who? How often?

**Workarounds** (this is where the gold is)
8. Do you keep your own list, spreadsheet, sticky notes, or inbox folder for this? Show me.
9. What would break if you were out for two weeks?
10. What do new people get wrong in their first month?

**Their view**
11. If you could change one thing about how this works, what would it be?
12. Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?

## 3. What to capture after each conversation (10 minutes)

| Field | Note |
|---|---|
| Steps they described | in order |
| Tools touched | |
| Waits and stalls | where, and how long |
| Personal workarounds | list, spreadsheet, memory |
| Decisions routed to someone else | to whom |
| Information re-entered | from where, to where |
| Quotes worth using | exact words |
| Surprises | |
