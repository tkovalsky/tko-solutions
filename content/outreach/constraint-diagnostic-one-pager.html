Constraint Diagnostic

What is actually slowing the business down, and what is the smallest useful fix?

Most growing businesses already have the tools. What they're missing is the layer that decides what happens next: who owns each lead or job, what state it's in, and what's due. When nothing decides, people do it from memory, usually the owner.

It's a fit when

  • Leads, requests, or jobs come in but don't move consistently.
  • The owner or COO is still the default router for routine decisions.
  • The CRM holds records but doesn't guide the work.
  • Handoffs depend on messages, spreadsheets, and memory.
  • A key person is leaving, or the business has outgrown how it was set up.

What you get

  • A plain-language diagnosis of where the work breaks down
  • A map of the workflow, handoffs, systems, and decision owners
  • The specific rules, states, or connections that are missing
  • A focused specification and fixed price for the first build
  • A simple baseline for judging whether the change helped
  • A working session to decide what to do next

How it runs

Week 1 · Listen and lookConversations with the people doing the work, plus a walkthrough of the tools, handoffs, and records around one important flow.
Week 2 · Follow the workFollow the work end to end. Find where it stalls, which decisions repeat, and who is compensating by hand.
Readout · DecideThe diagnosis, a fixed-price plan for the first fix, a starting measure, and a plain recommendation, including "leave it alone."

What I've built

RachelOS: a system I designed, built, and run for an independent South Florida real-estate operation where one person held the whole business in her head. It sits beside the existing tools and produces one ranked list each morning of who needs attention and why. A person approves every message before it goes to a client.
